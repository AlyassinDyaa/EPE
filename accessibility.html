Accessibility statement

East Park Entertainment exists to give creators a bigger audience. That only works if everyone can read, browse and get in touch, whatever device, assistive technology or setup they use.

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA across the public site and the team dashboard.

What we've built in

  • Full keyboard support, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to main content" link on every page.
  • Semantic headings, landmarks and labels so screen readers (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, TalkBack) can navigate easily.
  • Colour contrast of at least 4.5:1 for body text, plus an optional high-contrast mode.
  • Respect for your system's dark mode and reduced-motion settings.
  • Animations and scrolling text that can be paused or turned off.
  • Atkinson Hyperlegible, a typeface designed for low-vision readers, for all body text.
  • Layouts that reflow from 320px phones to wide desktop screens and support 400% zoom without horizontal scrolling.
  • Form fields with visible labels, clear instructions and error messages that are announced to screen readers.
  • Touch targets of at least 44 × 44 pixels.

Personalise the site

Open the (the yellow button at the top of every page) to change:

  • Text size, in four steps
  • Light or dark theme
  • High contrast
  • Reduced motion
  • Readable spacing (wider letter, word and line spacing)
  • Highlighted links

Your choices are saved on your device and apply across the whole site.

Known limitations

  • Creator portraits are generated illustrations, not photos. They're decorative and hidden from screen readers. The person's name is always given as text.
  • Any audio or video interviews we publish will come with full transcripts. If you find one without a transcript, please let us know.

Feedback and contact

If anything on this site is hard to use, or you need content in a different format, tell us:

Email: access@eastparkentertainment.com

We aim to reply within 5 working days.

How we test

We test with automated checks, keyboard-only navigation, screen readers on desktop and mobile, 200% and 400% browser zoom, and Windows High Contrast / forced-colours mode. We review this statement at least every six months.
